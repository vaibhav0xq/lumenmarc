## Tokenized Stocks FAQs

Prospectus

What is a tokenized stock?

An onchain token that gives eligible users direct economic exposure to a listed stock. Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are issued as B20 tokens by a regulated issuer, backed 1:1 by a real underlying share held in regulated custody.

What are Coinbase Tokenized Stocks?

Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are shares in individual US companies brought onchain. Coinbase is the issuer, licensed and authorized by the Abu Dhabi Global Market's Financial Services Regulatory Authority and a regulated broker and custodian, holds them in a bankruptcy-remote structure, in a trust for token holders. Base is the network the tokens run on: they are issued as B20 tokens, can be held in a self-custodial wallet, and can be traded and used across DeFi.

How is this different from other tokenized stock products?

Coinbase Tokenized Stocks give you exposure to a real underlying share. Holders have a direct, senior claim on the underlying equity. Some other products offer a synthetic, a debt claim, or a derivative wrapper rather than a claim on a real share.

Who is eligible?

Coinbase Tokenized Stocks are offered under Regulation S and are not available in every market. They are not available to persons in the US and other restricted jurisdictions.

How are dividends and splits handled?

Through an onchain multiplier rather than by changing your token balance. Your raw token count stays the same while the multiplier scales to reflect dividends (reinvested after applicable withholding and fees) and splits. Your share-equivalent value updates without breaking DeFi positions.

Who can mint or redeem tokens?

Primary minting and redemption are limited to KYC-onboarded institutional partners and Authorized Participants, using the same create-and-redeem model as traditional ETFs and ADRs.

What are the tokenized stocks trading hours?

The tokens live onchain and can be traded around the clock including market holidays.
